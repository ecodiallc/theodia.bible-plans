# Theodia Bible Plans

A repository of downloadable Bible reading plans for the Theodia app.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Five-Day-Introduction-To-Jesus.bible-plan.json` | A simple five-day reading plan that walks through key passages revealing who Jesus is, why He came, what He taught, how He died and rose again, and what it means to follow Him |
| `Read-The-Bible-In-A-Year.bible-plan.json` | A one-year reading plan that walks through every chapter of the Protestant Bible from Genesis to Revelation in canonical order, averaging about three chapters per day |
| `Read-The-New-Testament-In-A-Year.bible-plan.json` | A one-year daily reading plan that walks through every chapter of the New Testament in canonical order from Matthew to Revelation, with built-in rest days for reflection and catch-up |
| `Read-The-Old-Testament-In-A-Year-Tanakh.bible-plan.json` | A one-year reading plan through the Old Testament in the Jewish Tanakh order: Torah, Prophets, and Writings. Every chapter is read once, averaging about three chapters per day |
| `Read-The-Bible-In-3-Years-Chronological.bible-plan.json` | A three-year chronological reading plan that walks through Scripture in the order events likely occurred, interleaving prophetic books with the historical accounts of the kings and eras they address |

## Package Index

`index.json` lists all available packages with metadata, including the file path, display name, description, author, tradition, tags, and version.
